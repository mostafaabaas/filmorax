05 — FAQ

What brands ask before their first production project

What is Filmorax?

It is the cinematic and advertising production arm of influUGC, the creative marketing and media production agency in Agouza (est. 2020). We produce for brands across Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf: commercials, brand films, cinematic content, product videos and event coverage — from concept to final delivery.

What types of production do you handle?

Six main types: commercial production for TV and social, brand films, cinematic content for social platforms, product videos, event coverage and aftermovies, and full post-production — editing, colour grading, sound and motion graphics.

Can you start from the idea and script, or do you need a ready brief?

Both. We can start from zero — concept, visual treatment and script built around your brand's goal — or you can bring a ready brief or script and we execute it fully: casting, locations, shoot and post-production.

Do you produce in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, or only Egypt?

We produce across Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. The influUGC team has delivered work for clients in both markets, including a corporate video for flynas (2023) and work for Riyadh Season and the Saudi Tourism Authority, and we arrange crews and locations around each project's city.

What does post-production include?

Our post-production covers everything: editing, colour grading and correction, sound design and mixing, motion graphics and titles, subtitling and localisation, and final delivery in every platform's versions and sizes — from the TV master to reels and stories cuts.

How do we start a production project with you?

It starts with a free consultation — book via the booking page or message us on WhatsApp. We listen to the goal, the product and the market, then come back with a written treatment and a production quote — no prices are published; every project is quoted by its scale and requirements.
