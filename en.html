03 · Production guides

Understand production before you buy it

Three focused guides from real shoot days — the key points are complete here, and you can apply them with any production company.

The brief

The production brief: the document everything is built on

A shoot day is priced, cast and scheduled from the brief. Every missing answer in it becomes an expensive decision taken on the day itself.

  • One objective and one message the crew can test every scene against
  • Final placements decided first — they set the formats and durations
  • A written deliverables list: durations, versions and languages, not assumptions
  • A declared approval chain: who signs off treatment, casting and final cut

Run it with a ready team? Book a free consultation

The shoot day

One full shoot day: a temporary factory running 12 hours

Before the first shot there is setup, lighting and rehearsal; after the last shot there is no film — only raw material the film is built from in post.

  • The schedule is ordered by location and daylight, not script order
  • A full crew: direction, camera, lighting, sound, art and cast
  • The call sheet is the law of the day — overtime is a decision, not a drift
  • Footage is backed up twice before anyone leaves the location

Have a shoot day coming? Book a free consultation

Rights

Usage rights: the film is one thing, the faces in it are another

Paying for production does not mean owning every use of the film forever. Talent and music rights are licensed by media, territory and duration.

  • Separate the production deliverable from talent image and voice rights
  • The agreement defines: media, territory, duration and category exclusivity
  • Library music and stock carry their own licence terms
  • Usage is written before casting — renegotiating after success costs more

04 · FAQ

Before you book a first production project

Straight answers to the questions that come up before a first engagement — no generalities.

What is the difference between a commercial and a brand film?

A commercial sells one message in a short runtime and is measured by direct response; a brand film builds an image and a story around the company and is measured by long-term impression. Your objective decides the format — and if the objective is unclear, we start with a consultation to settle the right format before any quoting.

Do I need to arrive with a finished script?

No. You can start from a simple brief — objective, audience and product — and we build the treatment and script on it. If you already have an approved script or concept, we start straight at pre-production: casting, locations and the shoot schedule.

Is one shoot day enough for my project?

It depends on the number of scenes, locations and products. We work it out with you in pre-production with a clear shot list, and we will tell you honestly if the content needs two days instead of squeezing it into one and delivering it short.

Do you deliver versions for every platform?

Yes. Delivery is a main master plus versions in every agreed size and duration: TV, YouTube, Reels, TikTok and Stories — all written into the deliverables list before the shoot, not decided after it.

How are video and talent usage rights handled?

They are written into the production agreement before filming: the media, the territory, the duration and category exclusivity. That way the campaign can run for its full planned term without a surprise renewal or a renegotiation after success.

How is pricing decided?

There are no published prices because every production is different. After the free consultation or your brief, you receive a detailed written quote: shoot days, post days and deliverables — with no hidden line items.
